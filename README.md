# Window Dance AI Generator

Upload one photo of one person, or one pet, and get a 12-second vertical video that looks like it was secretly filmed at night from across the street: the phone zooms into the only lit window of a dark apartment block, the person dances alone next to a small speaker, notices the camera, grins, and pulls the curtain shut. A pet freezes instead and just stares.

## What it makes

- 12 seconds, 9:16 vertical, 720p, sized for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- The face and the clothes in your photo stay yours; the building, street, room and light are generated
- Original AI instrumental plus room sound, no third-party audio baked in
- MP4 download and GIF export, no watermark
- Generation takes about 3 to 5 minutes

## How it works

1. Upload one photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10MB). A free AI photo check runs before payment and rejects photos of minors and obvious celebrities.
2. Pick a credit pack: 1 credit $9.99, 3 credits $19.99. One-time purchase, no subscription, credits never expire.
3. The shot is generated with Seedance 2.0 from illustrated reference images built off your photo.
4. The video lands in My videos and in your email. No account needed; guests check out and get the file by email.

## Limits, stated plainly

- One person or one pet per video. No duo version, no person-plus-pet.
- People must be 18 or older, and only photos you have the right to use.
- No free credits and no trial. A failed generation returns the credit automatically, and unused credits are refundable within 7 days.
- Everything is AI-generated. Keep the AI label when you post, and never film real people through their windows.
