Window Dance AI

One photo in, a 12-second window down the street out.

The Window Dance trend is a twelve-second clip that looks like it was filmed secretly at night from across the street: the camera finds the one lit window in a dark building, zooms in on somebody dancing alone next to a small speaker, gets noticed, gets a guilty grin, and then the curtain closes. This tool builds that whole shot from a single photo of one person or one pet.

Try it at windowdance.com

What you actually get

  • 12 seconds, vertical 9:16, 720p, MP4 download plus a GIF export, no watermark
  • Your face, hair and outfit survive the shot, or your pet's fur and markings
  • An original AI instrumental and room tone, so nothing gets muted for copyright
  • Usually 3 to 5 minutes, straight in the browser, no app and no account
  • If a generation fails, the credit comes back automatically

What it costs

One-time credit packs, no subscription: $9.99 for one video, $19.99 for three. A free AI photo check runs before you pay, and unused credits are refundable for seven days. There is no free trial and no free tier.

What it will not do

One photograph means one subject. There is no two-person version and no person-with-pet version, because the model needs a single clear subject to keep the face or the markings intact. Adults only, and only photos you have the right to use. The joke is pretending to spy on somebody; it is not a tool for actually filming people through their windows.

How it differs from the CapCut template

The template swaps a face onto one fixed clip, so every video shares the same room, clothes and moves, and it only works on people, not pets. This generates the scene around your own picture instead, keeps your own clothes, handles cats and dogs, and hands back a postable file with no watermark.
